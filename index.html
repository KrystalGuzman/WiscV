Not a substitute for the published test. This tool performs the arithmetic of WISC‑V scoring from scaled scores you enter. It contains no WISC‑V test items and no copyrighted norms tables. Composite conversions are estimated from an explicit psychometric model (methodology), so they will differ from the official tables by a point or two. Administration, scoring and interpretation of the WISC‑V require appropriate professional qualifications.
